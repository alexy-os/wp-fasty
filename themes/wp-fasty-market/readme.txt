Marketplace child theme of Latte for selling ready-made WordPress sites, themes and plugins.

== Description ==

WP FastY Market turns Latte into a digital-goods marketplace: home with search and categories, catalog with filters and sorting, item pages with live preview, Regular/Extended licenses, changelog, compatibility, support and reviews, author pages, a signed-cookie cart and a checkout for digital delivery that creates orders.

Child theme of Latte (parent theme required). Product and Order post types are registered by the companion plugin "WP FastY Types". Colors come from design tokens (assets/css/tokens.css, light and dark), icons are Lucide, UI is built from UI8Kit Latte primitives. Russian translation included.
